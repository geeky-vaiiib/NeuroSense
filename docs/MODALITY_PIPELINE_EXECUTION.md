## Explicit input policies and limitations

Speech: five-second minimum unchanged; acoustic features at 22.05 kHz; emotion gets native decoded audio and the original shared 16 kHz/log-Mel/chunk-mean path. `speech-input-hygiene-v1` rejects silence, median spectral flatness >=0.5, or <0.5 seconds of pYIN voiced-frame evidence before emotion inference. Measurements survive. Thresholds are conservative engineering policy choices, **not validated speech detection or model accuracy boundaries**. Spectral flatness measures noise-like versus tonal spectra, not speech: [librosa documentation](https://librosa.org/doc/0.11.0/generated/librosa.feature.spectral_flatness.html), accessed 2026-11-06. A deterministic white-noise test exposed false positive voiced frames from pYIN; flatness covers that particular noise case. Tones/music, whispered speech, other noise/device/language conditions remain limitations. Clipped-sample fraction is measured; there is no validated clipping acceptance threshold. Generated-tone E2E is numeric integration coverage only.

Face: `face-input-hygiene-v1` rejects >1 detected face, empty crop, crop mean outside [20,235] on the 0–255 grayscale scale, or Laplacian variance <10 after 64x64 resizing before CLAHE. These are documented engineering heuristics, **not validated face/emotion quality boundaries**. Retain all actual image measurements and exact failure reason. Detector misses, different camera processing and moderate blur still require operator evaluation; not every bad input is detectable. CNN input remains the shared detected-face + 10% margin + grayscale64 + CLAHE + [-1,1] normalization. Detection count adds no identity recognition.

Gaze: OpenFace radians, preserved timing, frozen quality/domain gates and train normalization unchanged. Descriptive degrees are not calibrated screen position, fixation or eye contact. Adult-webcam versus historical child-source transfer remains unvalidated. An out-of-domain rejection is not successful model inference. Eye-tracker pixel-schema workflow remains separate. No diagnostic fusion or ASD speech classifier was added.
