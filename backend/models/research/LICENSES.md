# Research model attribution and permitted scope

These are newly trained local research artifacts, not clinically validated classifiers. They never determine the screening result. No source recordings/images/private participant manifests are distributed here. Dataset authors do not endorse NeuroSense.

* **facial-v1** (`weights.pt`, `baseline.json`, metadata): trained on V_Expression v1, Hoang-Anh Nguyen The, Ha Nguyen Dang and Chau Ma Thi (2025), DOI https://doi.org/10.17632/9gr9cn3bbn.1, CC BY 4.0 https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/. Download used Aleksandar Tomašević's attributed course v1.0 redistribution https://github.com/atomashevic/emotion-course-data: EXIF-oriented, resized/re-encoded JPEGs with subject splits and source/copy hashes. NeuroSense changes: detected-face crop, grayscale64/CLAHE, training-only augmentation, HOG baseline and CNN training/evaluation. Model artifacts offered under CC BY 4.0 with this attribution. Intended posed-expression categories, not internal feelings or ASD.
* **speech-v1** (`weights.pt`, `baseline.json`, metadata): Livingstone, S. R., & Russo, F. A. (2018), RAVDESS v1.0.0, https://zenodo.org/records/1188976; paper https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0196391. **CC BY-NC-SA 4.0** https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/. Noncommercial research only. These derived model artifacts are distributed under that same licence. NeuroSense changes: 16kHz mono, silence trimming, <=4s chunks, log-Mel/MFCC features, speaker-disjoint splits and LR/CNN training/evaluation. Commercial use requires separate permission; no commercial deployment is authorized. Targets are acted emotions, not autism.
* **gaze-v1** (`weights.pt`, `baseline.json`, metadata): Cilia, F., Carette, R., Elbattah, M., Guérin, J., & Dequen, G. (2022), Eye-Tracking Dataset to Support the Research on Autism Spectrum Disorder, Figshare v1 https://doi.org/10.6084/m9.figshare.20113592, CC BY 4.0. NeuroSense changes: participant grouping, documented exclusions, 20Hz pixel-coordinate windows, centring, train-only normalization and independent statistics/BiLSTM/context/order comparisons. Model artifacts offered under CC BY 4.0 with attribution. **Offline only**: eye-tracker screen pixels are incompatible with OpenFace webcam angles. Source labels/clinical ascertainment are not independently verified. DASD was not used or retrained.

The dataset licences do not grant instrument rights, confer diagnostic validity, verify participant identity or establish consent for new collection.
